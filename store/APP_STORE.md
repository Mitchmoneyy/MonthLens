## Description

```
MonthLens is a monthly business review for online business owners. It reads like a CFO and an ad analyst sat down with last month's numbers, not like a spreadsheet.

SEE WHERE THE MONEY WENT
• Revenue, every cost as dollars and % of revenue, profit per sale, cash runway
• A 0–100 health score with a one-line verdict
• A clean profit & loss statement you can export as a PDF
• Tap any number to see exactly how it was calculated

BRING YOUR NUMBERS ANY WAY YOU HAVE THEM
• Screenshots of Stripe, Shopify, Ads Manager or your bank, read on your phone
• CSV, PDF and JSON exports, including messy ones
• Pasted text, eight plain-language questions, or manual entry
• Nothing is applied until you approve it

FIND THE ADS THAT PAY
• Compare video, static, carousel, UGC, reels, search, shopping and more
• Profit after ad cost for every format, not just ROAS
• Automatic labels: best ROAS, cheapest CAC, money loser
• A budget mixer that finds a better split for the same spend

TRACK YOUR UGC CREATORS
• Scan a creator's profile screenshot or post export
• Post count, best post, typical views, engagement and cost per 1,000 views

PLAN NEXT MONTH
• Fixes ranked by how much profit they add each month
• What-if sliders for price, ad spend, conversion, churn and costs
• A first-week checklist and a projected profit
• Break-even calculator and runway explorer

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No ads, no tracking, no account needed
• Your numbers stay on your device; an optional account syncs them between your phone and computer
• Delete your account any time, right in the app

Also works in any browser on your computer at mitchmoneyy.github.io/MonthLens.
```

## App Privacy (App Store Connect → App Privacy)

Tracking: **No**, the app does not track people.

Data collected, **only when someone creates the optional account**:

| Data type | Used for | Linked to the person | Used for tracking |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Contact Info → Email Address | App Functionality | Yes | No |
| User Content → Other User Content (the business figures they save) | App Functionality | Yes | No |

Nothing else: no analytics, no diagnostics, no identifiers, no location.
